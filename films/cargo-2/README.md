# Cargo kinetic type 2: «21 аймгийн 330 суманд хүргэнэ»

3.8 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, transparent background. Green #008c00, white strokes, Montserrat 900.

21 and 330 rise and count up in a right-aligned column while their words slide in beside them,
ХҮРГЭНЭ rises letter by letter, and a dashed white route draws out to a pin. Everything slides
out to the left and the clip ends on an empty frame.
