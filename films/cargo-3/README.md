# Cargo kinetic type 3: «100гр тутамд ₮270»

3.4 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, transparent background. Green #008c00, white strokes, Montserrat 900.

100ГР ТУТАМД rises letter by letter, a white price tag draws itself with a punched hole and
string, ₮ springs in and the price counts up to 270. The tag swings down from its hole and drops
away as the unit line lifts off; the clip ends on an empty frame.
